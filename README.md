# Sales Performance Analysis | SQL + Excel

A end-to-end sales operations analysis built to answer the kinds of questions a Sales Ops or Revenue Operations team deals with weekly.

## Business Questions Answered
- Which reps are hitting quota and which are at risk?
- Where is pipeline stalling across the funnel stages?
- Which products drive the most revenue and how are discounts affecting deal size?
- How does closed revenue trend month over month year over year?

## Tools Used
- SQL (SQLite) for querying and structuring the data
- Excel for dashboard creation, charts, and rep scorecard with conditional formatting

## What the Analysis Covers
- Rep performance scorecard with quota attainment flagged by conditional formatting
- Monthly revenue trend comparing 2023 vs 2024
- Pipeline funnel breakdown by stage and value
- Product revenue mix with average discount analysis
- Regional win rate comparison

## Dataset
600 synthetic sales opportunities modeled after a realistic B2B pipeline, generated to simulate the kind of data typically housed in a CRM like Salesforce.
